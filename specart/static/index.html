SpecArt

Give it any image. It searches for the ZX Spectrum picture that looks most like it, runs that picture on the emulator, and measures how close the emulator's screen is to your original.

Drop an image here, or click to choose JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, WebP ... any size or colour depth
The emulator window is restarted for each picture.
